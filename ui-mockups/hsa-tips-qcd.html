◆
QCDs save you $61,400 in lifetime taxes (today's $)
From age 72, $25,000 a year of your giving goes straight from your IRA to charity. That covers 64% of your required withdrawals, lowers taxable Social Security, and keeps you out of IRMAA tier 1 in 9 years. Same withdrawal plan, compared with paying the same gifts in cash.
✚
Your HSA covers Medicare premiums and medical bills from 65
$184,000 of medical costs paid tax-free over the plan. Contributing $9,750 a year on a bronze plan before 65 lowers your ACA income by the same amount and raises your subsidy by about $3,100 a year.
